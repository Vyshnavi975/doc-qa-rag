Solstice Robotics: Company Overview

Solstice Robotics was founded in 2019 in Portland, Oregon by engineers Mara Ilhan and
Devon Kessler. The company designs small autonomous robots that help greenhouses and
indoor farms monitor plant health without constant human supervision. Solstice Robotics
is a private company and has not taken on outside investors; it has grown entirely from
customer revenue since its first product shipped in 2020.

The company's mission is to make precision agriculture affordable for small and mid-sized
growers, not just large industrial farms. Solstice Robotics currently employs 42 people
across two offices: an engineering and manufacturing site in Portland, Oregon, and a
smaller customer support office in Guelph, Ontario, Canada. The Guelph office opened in
2022 to better serve the company's growing base of Canadian greenhouse customers.

Solstice Robotics' flagship product line is called the Scout series, a family of small
wheeled robots that roll along greenhouse benches and use onboard cameras and sensors to
check soil moisture, leaf color, and pest activity. Data collected by Scout robots is
uploaded to a companion dashboard called Solstice Hub, which growers use to spot problems
early. As of the start of 2026, Solstice Robotics has shipped over 3,100 Scout robots to
customers in the United States, Canada, and the Netherlands.

The company's CEO is Mara Ilhan, who previously worked as a mechanical engineer at an
industrial robotics firm before co-founding Solstice. Devon Kessler serves as Chief
Technology Officer and leads the software and machine learning team. Solstice Robotics
was named one of Oregon's fastest-growing small manufacturers in 2024 by a regional
business journal, based on year-over-year revenue growth.

Solstice Robotics' headquarters address is 1180 Foundry Lane, Portland, Oregon. General
inquiries can be sent to hello@solsticerobotics.example, and the company's support line
is described in a separate FAQ document.
